## 2. The service

KiFéKoi helps a household share its mental load: each task is broken down into
three phases — anticipate, plan, execute — and the app computes the share carried
by each person.

Two modes, chosen by the user:

- **On this device**: everything stays on the phone. No account, no server.
  **A household lost with the phone is lost for good**: there is no remote backup
  and we can restore nothing.
- **Shared**: the household is hosted on a server and reachable from several
  devices.

---

## 3. Account and invitation

Shared mode creates an account with no e-mail and no password. **That account
lives in the device's keychain**: losing it means losing access to the household,
with no recovery procedure. This is a consequence of asking for no personal data
at sign-up.

Joining a household uses an invitation code. **Anyone holding the code can join
the household** and see all of its content, including history and check-in
answers. Share it only with the people concerned.
